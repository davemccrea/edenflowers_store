## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
A marketing plan for the three months after the new website's launch and Jennie's return from parental leave (Nov 2026 to early Feb 2027), aimed at steady weekly orders.

**Why:** The shop was closed for months and the new site is unknown to most past customers. Without a deliberate plan, the relaunch spends its one moment of news on a single email and the Christmas season goes to other florists.

## Context (2026-11-01)
- Reopening email drafted in MailerLite (campaign reopening-2026) to the Reopening group (293 recent customers), sv+fi, 25% newsletter offer until 16.11.2026 (TASK-152, TASK-153). It is a one-off: the old site collected no marketing consent.
- No mailing list yet beyond that. Instagram has about 1750 followers, plus a Facebook page. No Google Business Profile yet.
- Jennie has about 1-2 h a week for marketing; David sets up the systems and drafts emails.
- Umami tracks orders, subscriptions and sign-ups by source (TASK-147).

## Plan
- **Goal:** steady weekly orders. **Metric:** orders per week, checked weekly. The first two weeks after the email are the baseline; then agree a target for February.
- **Lead offer:** the weekly bouquet (changed from flower subscriptions, 2026-11-01). Channels sell directly; newsletter growth is a side effect.
- **Instagram:** one post a week plus stories, captions in Jennie's own voice. About 20-50 EUR a month boosting the best post to Vaasa, linking to whatever the post shows. Links carry UTM tags so Umami can tell posts and boosts apart.
- **Newsletter:** monthly in Jennie's voice, plus short Christmas extras. Only to subscribers.
- **Google:** Business Profile set up in November (TASK-73), then the review ask in the Delivered email (TASK-159).

## Calendar
- **Tue 3.11 (about):** send the reopening email as drafted; same day an 'I'm back, new website' post and stories, boosted.
- **16.11:** 25% offer ends; Jennie sets the welcome percent back.
- **5.11:** Reel 1 sells the weekly bouquet for Father's Day (Sun 8.11).
- **By 20.11:** grill TASK-159 and TASK-161, which TASK-163 and the January pages wait on.
- **November:** Google Business Profile; promote the Christmas course two weeks before it runs.
- **23-29.11:** Jennie prepares and schedules December's four posts (vouchers, Independence Day 6.12, Lucia 13.12, Christmas deadline) in Meta Business Suite, before the Christmas rush.
- **Early December:** gift vouchers live (TASK-160, simple version), announced by newsletter and post, also sold as 'a few months of flowers' for gift subscriptions. Christmas flowers and the last order day pushed.
- **January:** the weekly bouquet through the dark winter, with having it every week as the next step; steer voucher recipients there. Occasion pages (TASK-161) live by mid-January. Two normal weeks, 11.1-24.1, set the orders-per-week target on 25.1.
- **By 29.1:** check Valentine's deliveries (Sun 14.2) for Sat 13.2 and Sun 14.2.
- **February:** Valentine's Day push, partly as the friends' day (Vändagen/Ystävänpäivä); review results against the target.

Review with the marketing skills (marketing-plan AARRR lens, 2026-11-01). Gaps found, each turned into a ticket:
- Activation: the site doesn't link to Instagram or Facebook, and the bio link isn't tagged (TASK-162.2). Facebook was in the plan's audience but had no role; it now gets the launch post via Meta Business Suite (TASK-162.1).
- Acquisition: the launch post for the 1750 followers had no ticket (TASK-162.1). Every delivered bouquet reaches a likely next customer, so a card in each order (TASK-162.3).
- Revenue: subscriptions are the lead offer, but nothing asks a one-off weekly bouquet buyer to subscribe (TASK-163, a spec of its own).
- Measurement: a saved weekly view in Umami plus rules for judging boosts (TASK-162.5). Custom Orders are not in Umami, so the weekly number counts both. The baseline window (3.11-16.11) overlaps the 25% offer and will overstate a normal week; decide in TASK-162.5.
- Timing: Finnish companies settle Christmas gifts by mid to late November, so vouchers live in early December come late for corporate buyers (noted on TASK-160).
Considered and skipped: abandoned-cart and win-back emails (the 293 have no consent, and the site's own buyers are too few yet); directories beyond Google (low value now); a referral programme (word of mouth and reviews come first).
