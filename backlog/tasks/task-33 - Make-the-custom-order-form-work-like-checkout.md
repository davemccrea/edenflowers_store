## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Jennie's custom order form asks the same questions, in the same shape, as the customer checkout, and never stops her saving an order she's taking over the phone.

**Why:** Jennie fills in custom orders while talking to a customer. A form that differs from checkout, refuses to save over one missing detail, or hides the payment choice slows her down and leads to mistakes.

The time of day for an order is a spec of its own (see the time-window task), as it will later reach checkout.
