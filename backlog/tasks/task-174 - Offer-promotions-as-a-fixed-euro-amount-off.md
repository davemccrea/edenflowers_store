## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Promotions can take off a fixed amount (e.g. €10) instead of a percentage.

**Why:** On a bouquet of about €50, '€10 off' feels bigger than '15% off' (€7.50). A later pass-it-on batch (TASK-165) could test it against the 15% batch, since each batch has its own name and its uses can be compared.

Open: whether it's worth it before the first batch's results are in; how a fixed amount meets a cart smaller than it, delivery fees and receipts/VAT.
