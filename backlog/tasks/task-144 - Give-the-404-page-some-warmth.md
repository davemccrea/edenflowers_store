## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The 404 page is the only storefront page without a flower; it's a plain apology with two buttons.

**Why:** a customer who lands on a dead link (old bookmark, mistyped URL, a link from the old site) gets the coldest page on the site at a moment they're already slightly lost. A touch of the shop's character turns a dead end into a small smile.

Idea from brainstorming: a wilted flower that perks up when the customer hovers or focuses the home button. Undecided: the exact treatment, whether the copy changes too, and whether to suggest popular products or the current season's bouquets.
