## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Jennie can edit a generated customer email's subject and full plain-text body before sending it from admin, replacing the separate optional note field.

**Why:** A note only adds a paragraph; Jennie sometimes needs to correct or rewrite the actual message. Editing the email she is about to send gives her control over its wording, including the language of manual course reminders, without requiring a code change.

## Current behaviour and finish line
Admin forms generate an email preview from the order, booking or subscription. Jennie's optional message is inserted after the opening paragraph. The final email is regenerated by its sender, so the preview itself cannot be edited. The feature is complete when admin-initiated customer emails use an editable generated draft, and delivery and retries retain the final composed wording.

## Proposed scope
- Inventory every customer email Jennie explicitly triggers: standalone send/resend actions and optional emails accompanying order entry/editing, manual bookings, recording payment, cancellation, refunds, fulfillment and subscription cancellation. Include the manual course-reminder drawer introduced in commit 8be8764c.
- Replace the optional customer-note input with editable subject and body fields, initially populated by the existing builder in the customer's language. Keep the current optional-send control on actions that have one.
- Until Jennie edits either email field, regenerate the draft as the underlying form changes. Once she edits either field, preserve both fields through form validation, other form changes and temporarily unticking email sending. Never silently overwrite an edited draft.
- Provide Reset to original to regenerate both fields from the current form/record and customer language. Cancelling the composer sends nothing. Edits apply to this send only.
- Preserve the existing sender, recipient, Bcc and attachments as system-managed values. Receipt PDFs and delivery photos remain attached under the existing rules.
- Resolve any payment link that does not exist until the business action saves against the saved record. Dummy preview URLs must never be delivered. The exact editing/resolution behaviour for system-managed links needs triage.
- Submit composed subject/body through the domain action, not a LiveView-only mail send. Queue the final text together with the successful business action where that action uses background delivery. Retries use the composed snapshot, not a newly generated email from later templates or record values. Failed email delivery must not undo a committed payment.
- Record the final subject/body actually delivered with the related email history, so Jennie can see what the customer received. Define the logging approach for records that currently lack order-style email history.
- For bulk course reminders, generate one draft per confirmed booking in that booking's language. Edits to one recipient's draft must not overwrite another recipient's language, name, amounts or wording. Recheck booking eligibility when sending; cancelled or pending bookings receive nothing. No automatic translation of Jennie's edits is proposed.

## Out of scope
- Editing the shared templates or translations for future emails.
- Rich text, HTML editing, saved reusable drafts, or draft recovery across browser visits.
- Editing recipients, sender, Bcc or attachments.
- Changes to automatically triggered emails, such as website checkout confirmations, subscription charges, authentication codes or routine scheduled notices.
- Changing course-payment totals or labels from item 4 of the 3 November discussion; that remains a separate decision.

## Triage remaining
This is a proposed feature, not an implementation-ready agreement. Confirm the entry-point inventory and how the composer fits into business-action forms; the bulk reminder editing experience; required subject/body validation and limits; payment-link editing/resolution; and how cancellation reasons/refund notes remain useful business records when the customer-note field is removed. Decide whether stale-information warnings are wanted; TASK-134 explicitly rejected them, and that older decision should not be silently reversed. This feature has not undergone the repository grilling workflow and stays needs-triage.

## Context
TASK-134, archived as wontfix on 27 October, explored full email composition but was closed because the optional note was considered enough. This new proposal revisits that decision and explicitly replaces the optional note. Treat TASK-134 as prior context, not a completed implementation or a dependency. The email cleanup in 645bc6d3 is already committed and should remain independent of this feature.

Relevant code: lib/edenflowers_web/features/admin/email_drawer.ex, drawers.ex, drawer_preview.ex and course_detail_live.ex; lib/edenflowers/email/preview.ex; lib/edenflowers/payments/changes/queue_email.ex; order/course/subscription email actions and senders. ADR 0005 describes queued delivery; ADR 0006 currently prescribes the note-plus-preview flow and will need updating as part of implementation.

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 Every admin-initiated customer email entry point is inventoried; the agreed scope uses one shared subject/body composer instead of a separate optional customer-note input.
- [ ] #2 Initial drafts match the generated customer-language email. They follow form changes until edited; edited subject/body survive validation failures, other form changes and temporarily disabling email sending.
- [ ] #3 Reset to original regenerates subject/body from the current form/record and language. Cancelling or disabling optional email sends and queues no customer message.
- [ ] #4 Sending delivers the final composed subject/body, and background retries preserve that wording. Failed business actions queue no email, and a mail failure never loses a successfully recorded payment.
- [ ] #5 Sender, recipient, Bcc and attachment behaviour are preserved. Any required saved-record links resolve correctly, and dummy preview URLs cannot reach customers.
- [ ] #6 Bulk course reminders support separate drafts in each booking language, preserve recipient-specific details, and send only to eligible confirmed bookings.
- [ ] #7 The final delivered subject/body are retained in the agreed email history. Subject/body validation and send authorisation are enforced through domain actions.
- [ ] #8 Behavioural coverage verifies composed delivery, retries, draft preservation/reset, optional sending, bulk recipient eligibility and real payment-link resolution. Standard previews are reviewed in Swedish, Finnish and English, and ADR 0006 is updated to match the implemented composer.
