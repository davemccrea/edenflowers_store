## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Customers who buy the weekly bouquet as a one-off are offered the same bouquet as a subscription, at the moment they are happiest with it.

**Why:** Subscriptions are the lead offer of the relaunch plan (TASK-162), but nothing on the site or in the emails asks a one-off buyer to subscribe. Someone who has just received the weekly bouquet is the most likely subscriber the shop will ever have.

Rough shape (to be grilled):
- One line and a link in the Delivered / Left at the door email, only when the order held a subscribable product and the customer has no subscription.
- Or on the order confirmation page, or both.

Open: which moment, wording (sv first), whether it carries an offer, whether a promotion in a service email needs consent, and whether it combines with the review ask (TASK-159) without making the email feel like an advert.
