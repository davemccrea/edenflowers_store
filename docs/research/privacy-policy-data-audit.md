# Privacy-policy data audit

Scope: current working-tree source and configuration, not a production audit or legal sign-off. No production database, provider dashboard, live cookie inventory, processor contract or mailbox was inspected. TASK-116 is untriaged; this note does not implement it or approve its proposed retention periods.

## Verified processing

- Orders retain customer name/email and, for Custom Orders, phone/Instagram; Recipient name/phone; address, delivery instructions, card message, geocoded address/coordinates and distance; delivery-photo reference; Florist Note; commercial and payment references; email/cancellation history. Placed-order changes are copied into PaperTrail, including some email messages. Sources: `lib/edenflowers/orders/order.ex`, `lib/edenflowers/orders/order/admin.ex`.
- Checkout can create a customer identity before payment, without separate account registration. Identities retain name/email, newsletter preference/language, Stripe Customer ID and pending email-change data; sign-in uses email codes. Sources: `lib/edenflowers/accounts/user.ex`, `lib/edenflowers/orders/order/checkout.ex`.
- Subscriptions retain recurring schedule, Recipient/delivery/card-message details, Stripe references, card brand/last four/expiry, and change history. Sources: `lib/edenflowers/orders/subscription.ex`.
- Course records retain name/email, places, course/reference, language, pricing/payment/cancellation/email history. Payment records retain amounts, method, dates, Stripe identifiers, card brand/last four and notes. Sources: `lib/edenflowers/courses/course_registration.ex`, `lib/edenflowers/payments/payment.ex`.
- Contact/wedding forms are persisted as background-job arguments and emailed via Brevo to the sender with a copy to the shop. Wedding enquiries include date, venue, guest count, selections and estimate. They are not merely ephemeral because there is no separate enquiry table. Sources: `lib/edenflowers/contact_message.ex`, `lib/edenflowers/wedding_enquiry.ex`, their `send_emails.ex` workers, `lib/edenflowers/email.ex`, `config/runtime.exs`.

## Recipients and external services

- Stripe: online payment, saved cards and subscriptions. Customer creation sends name/email; payment metadata links purchases. The app stores identifiers/limited card details, not full card numbers or security codes. Sources: `lib/edenflowers/payments.ex`, `lib/edenflowers/accounts/changes/create_stripe_customer.ex`, `lib/edenflowers/external/stripe_api.ex`, `assets/js/stripe.js`.
- Brevo: transactional mail and newsletter. The configured nightly contact sync sends email, names, phone (where available), language, order count, latest order date and total spent for newsletter subscribers **and non-subscribing customers with a phone number**. Non-subscribers are not added to the newsletter list. Stated purpose is future/order texts; this code does not itself establish an implemented SMS flow or a lawful basis for sharing all statistics. Opt-out removes newsletter-list membership, not necessarily the Brevo contact. Sources: `lib/edenflowers/accounts/user.ex` (`brevo_contacts`), `lib/edenflowers/accounts/workers/sync_newsletter_list.ex`, `config/config.exs`, `config/runtime.exs`.
- HERE receives address searches and coordinates to geocode and calculate delivery distance. Mapbox receives Recipient coordinates for a static admin map; opening directions sends the coordinates or address to Google Maps. Sources: `lib/edenflowers/external/here_api.ex`, `lib/edenflowers_web/features/admin/order_detail_components.ex:697-719`.
- Cloudflare Tunnel is configured in front of the service. Hosting identity/location, Cloudflare settings and live infrastructure remain unverified. Sources: `infra/README.md`, `infra/roles/common/templates/docker-compose.yml.j2`.
- Accounting reports export purchase references, payments and expense documents for the accountant. External in-person/invoice payment services also need business confirmation: domain docs name Zettle, MobilePay and Zervant. Sources: `lib/edenflowers/accounting/monthly_report.ex`, `CONTEXT.md`, `docs/adr/0004-orders-are-the-sales-record.md`.
- Back-office expense processing fetches whole receipt/invoice documents from Papra and sends their bytes to Anthropic for extraction. This is not evidence that customer orders are sent to AI; assess personal data in uploaded expense documents and decide whether a supplier notice is also needed. Sources: `lib/edenflowers/expenses/changes/process.ex`, `lib/edenflowers/external/claude_api.ex`.

## Cookies and diagnostics: corrections to older task notes

- `_edenflowers_key` is a signed session cookie with a configured 30-day lifetime, for session state including cart/language/authentication. Source: `lib/edenflowers_web/endpoint.ex`, session plugs and authentication code.
- Stripe.js loads across pages through `assets/js/app.js:54-60`, not just payment pages. The loader does not disable advanced fraud signals. Do not promise payment-page-only Stripe cookies or that a banner is categorically unnecessary without a browser audit and Finnish cookie-law assessment.
- No dedicated audience analytics or advertising pixel was found in the inspected application entry points. This is not a finding of no tracking: Stripe documents device/activity signals, including time on pages and mouse activity, for fraud prevention. Primary source: [Stripe advanced fraud detection](https://docs.stripe.com/disputes/prevention/advanced-fraud-detection).
- ErrorTracker is local to the database and automatically attaches Phoenix/Oban integrations. HTTP error context can include forwarded IP, headers (including User-Agent), path/query and parsed parameters; LiveView exception context can include submitted event data; job exception context includes job arguments. There is no configured custom filter or pruning plugin. Thus "no IP/user-agent logging" and "enquiries disappear after seven days" are not supportable promises. Sources: `config/config.exs`; `deps/error_tracker/lib/error_tracker/application.ex`, `integrations/plug.ex:114-145`, `integrations/phoenix.ex`, `integrations/oban.ex`; `lib/edenflowers/error_tracker_log_handler.ex`.

## Retention actually configured

- Nightly cleanup only targets unplaced carts with no line items or PaymentIntent and over one day without an update. Populated abandoned checkouts are not generally purged. Source: `lib/edenflowers/orders/order/checkout.ex:44-70`.
- No general age-based deletion of placed orders, bookings, subscriptions, identities, their histories, delivery photos or enquiries in mailboxes was established. Cancellation preserves purchase history. Sources: relevant resource actions, `docs/adr/0004-orders-are-the-sales-record.md`, `docs/purchase-retention-rollout.md`.
- Oban terminal-job pruning is configured for seven days; it is not a blanket seven-day deletion guarantee for all queued data or copies in errors/mailboxes/backups. Source: `config/config.exs`, `deps/oban/lib/oban/plugins/pruner.ex`.
- Daily database dumps are pruned with `find -mtime +30` after a successful dump, on the same host. This is approximately a 30-day backup window, not a guaranteed exact deletion deadline; local deployment dumps and uploaded files need separate assessment. Source: `infra/roles/common/tasks/backups.yml`.

2. Approve and implement a category-specific retention schedule. Do not copy the old task's blanket "seven years required by law" proposal: Finnish Accounting Act chapter 2 section 10 distinguishes at least ten years for specified accounting records and at least six years from the end of the year in which the financial year ended for vouchers/business correspondence. The accountant must classify the store's records. This does not justify retaining delivery photos/card messages indefinitely. Primary source: [Accounting Act 1336/1997](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/1997/1336). Relevant Finnish statutory text was available in the page payload; direct EUR-Lex GDPR HTML retrieval returned no usable text in this environment.
3. Minimise Brevo non-subscriber attributes or document why each is necessary and its lawful basis. Determine whether newsletter segmentation actually uses purchase statistics.
4. Confirm hosting, mailbox, accountant, delivery helpers, payment providers, processor contracts, international transfers and safeguards. Code cannot establish that all data stays in the EEA or identify the applicable transfer mechanism.
5. Audit Stripe browser behaviour, cookies/storage/purposes/lifetimes and consent requirements. Review diagnostic redaction and retention, particularly sign-in codes/private link parameters and free-text forms.
6. Establish rights-request handling, deletion across copies/providers and restoration safeguards. Rights apply subject to conditions, and requests are normally answered within one month. Primary overview: [Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman: rights](https://tietosuoja.fi/en/rights-of-the-data-subject).
7. Assess GDPR Article 14 information for Recipients whose data comes from the Customer, including appropriate timing/exceptions. A checkout instruction to the Customer alone does not establish the shop's compliance.
8. Do not make a blanket "no automated decision-making" claim before checking Stripe Radar/risk settings and their effects on payment acceptance.

Suggested policy structure: controller/contact; people and data covered; purposes and legal bases (contract/pre-contract for Customers, legitimate interests for Recipient fulfillment/security with assessment, legal obligation for accounting, consent for newsletter); service recipients and transfers; cookies/security data; specific retention; rights and Finnish supervisory authority. Treat any draft as not publication-ready until the decisions above are resolved.
