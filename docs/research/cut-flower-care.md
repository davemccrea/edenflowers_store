# Cut-flower care for customers

Source: [RHS Advice Team — Cut flowers: cutting and conditioning](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/for-places/cut-flowers-conditioning), reviewed 2026-10-26.

The storefront FAQ condenses the RHS guidance into four everyday care points:

1. **Clean vase and fresh water.** “In the vase” recommends clean vases and regular water changes.
2. **Keep leaves out of the water.** “Conditioning plant material” says to remove foliage below water level to prevent rotting.
3. **Maintain and refresh the water.** “In the vase” recommends checking levels every couple of days and changing water and re-cutting stems every two to four days. The FAQ retains its existing two-to-three-day interval, within that range.
4. **Keep flowers cool and away from heat and ripe fruit.** “In the vase” recommends a cool position away from radiators and fires; “Problems” also recommends keeping arrangements away from ripe fruit.

These are selected for customers receiving prepared bouquets. At Jennie's request, cut-flower food and the separate initial stem-recutting point are omitted. Specialist conditioning treatments, harvesting advice and homemade preservatives are also omitted. The three-day photo deadline and replacement/refund conditions are the shop's policy, not RHS recommendations.
