# Warming up the site in Jennie's voice

Suggestions from 2026-11-01 for bringing the storefront's Swedish copy closer to how Jennie writes, based on [jennie-voice.md](jennie-voice.md). Every proposed line is taken from her own Instagram captions, with emojis removed and her spaced hyphens turned into commas or colons. Anything changed beyond that is marked *(adapted)*. "L782" means line 782 of [instagram-captions-2026-11-01.md](instagram-captions-2026-11-01.md).

Nothing here is applied yet. Jennie's Swedish becomes the master text; the English msgids and Finnish msgstrs are then aligned to it.

## Not site copy, but hers to use

- **Weekly bouquet product description** (in the admin): "vald av floristen" → "vald av mig". For the week's flowers, her caption shape "Veckans bukett innehåller…" fits the notice bar in TASK-150.
- **Order ahead before holidays** (notice bar or newsletter): **"Beställ din bukett i tid, gärna redan nu. Det hjälper mig att planera och köpa tillräckligt med blommor, samtidigt som jag undviker onödigt svinn."** (L252)

## Open questions

- **"dom" or "de"?** She writes "dom" (items 4, 6, 7). It's authentic, but it's informal on a fixed page.
- **"ni" or "du"?** Her captions switch between them. Items 5 and 13 mix both against the site's "ni" on wedding pages, so they may need aligning.
- **Finnish:** many of these posts have her own Finnish version. Use those for fi rather than translating fresh.

## Deliberately not recommended

- **Emoji in site copy.** Her one-emoji-per-paragraph habit suits Instagram and the newsletter, not page headings or buttons.
- **SEO meta descriptions** ("av Jennie på Eden Flowers"). Third person is normal there.
- **Finland-Swedish dialect** like "vettja" and "norpa" on fixed pages. They suit posts but would date quickly on a page.
